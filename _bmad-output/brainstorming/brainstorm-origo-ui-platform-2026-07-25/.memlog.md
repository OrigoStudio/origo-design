[technique] — started First Principles Thinking (Technique 5) on: what must metadata express to describe any business application?
[insight] (by coach) — Bedrock Fact #1 attempt: every business app is operations on data mediated by rules → implies CRUD platform risk
[insight] (by user) — Bedrock Fact #1 CORRECTION: every business app exists to model a business domain and help humans/systems make decisions within it — data is persistent representation, UI is one presentation, AI is one consumer/producer, the domain is the constant
[insight] (by user) — hierarchy: Business Domain → Capabilities → Rules → Objects → Interactions → Renderers — Angular/React Native are at the bottom, they are renderers not architecture
[insight] (by user) — capability is stable, interaction changes: "Approve Invoice" could be button, swipe, voice, chatbot, AI tool, keyboard shortcut
[idea] (by user) — BADL: Business Application Description Language as the core package @origo/core — Domain, Entities, Value Objects, Capabilities, Workflows, Business Rules, Permissions, Validations, Events, Commands, Queries, Navigation, Localization, Presentation Hints, Renderer Extensions
[insight] (by user) — no Angular, no React Native, no UI components in @origo/core — those belong in renderer packages: @origo/angular-renderer, @origo/react-native-renderer, etc.
[insight] (by user) — "Runtime Context" rejected: describes WHERE the app executes not WHAT it requires — core language should be ignorant of execution environments
[insight] (by user) — refined hierarchy: Business Domain → Capability Contract → Interaction Contract → Experience Adapter → Renderer
[insight] (by user) — Interaction Contract expresses WHAT the interaction needs (Confirmation Required, Authentication Level = Strong, Interruptible = No, Undo Allowed = No) — not HOW it appears (no modal, no dialog, no HTML)
[insight] (by user) — Experience Adapter resolves interaction contract to medium: web→modal, mobile→bottom sheet, voice→"Are you sure?", AI→structured challenge, CLI→[y/N]
[insight] (by user) — future-proofing argument: spatial computing (eye-tracking confirmation) / AR glasses (gesture confirmation) — if BADL says "Runtime = Mobile" it's already stale; it should say "Needs confirmation" and let the adapter resolve
[insight] (by user) — the real missing abstraction: every metadata language conflates Business Intent, Interaction Intent, and Presentation — BADL must separate them as distinct layers with single responsibility
[insight] (by user) — AI consumer implication: AI doesn't need Button/Modal/BottomSheet — only needs Capability + Confirmation Required + Reason Required + Strong Auth; can satisfy requirements through its own interaction protocol
[idea] (by user) — Business Outcomes as root: "Sell a product / Pay a supplier / Close accounting period / Hire an employee / Approve a loan" — everything else (entities, workflows, validations, permissions, UI) is in service of accomplishing that intent
[insight] (by user) — proposed apex hierarchy: Business Outcome → Capabilities → Business Rules → Interaction Requirements → Experience Adapters → Renderers
[insight] (by user) — "technologies evolve, but organizations will continue to pursue business outcomes" — that level of abstraction survives technology change
